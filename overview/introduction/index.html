What is Maximo Aviation?

Overview​

Maximo Aviation is an industry solution for the Maximo Manage application of the IBM Maximo Application Suite.

It is designed to manage the complex configuration and lifecycle of highly regulated, safety-critical, and configuration-dependent assets—such as found in aviation industry.

Key Functionality​

Maximo Aviation extends Maximo’s core asset management capabilities by introducing configuration-aware asset tracking and control. Its main features include:

  • Configuration Management: Track and manage the hierarchical structure and configuration of complex assets, including serialized components and assemblies.
  • Life-Limited Parts Tracking: Monitor usage and life limits of critical components to ensure compliance with safety and maintenance regulations.
  • Effectivity Management: Apply changes to assets based on specific conditions such as time, usage, or serial number ranges.
  • Change Control: Manage engineering changes and ensure traceability of modifications across the asset lifecycle.
  • Maintenance Planning Integration: Align configuration data with maintenance schedules to ensure accurate and timely servicing.
  • Compliance and Audit Readiness: Maintain a complete history of asset configurations and changes for regulatory compliance and audit purposes.

Benefits​

Implementing Maximo Aviation provides organizations with:

  • Improved Asset Visibility: Gain a detailed, real-time view of asset configurations and their operational status.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Ensure adherence to industry standards and regulations through robust tracking and documentation.
  • Reduced Operational Risk: Minimize the risk of failure or non-compliance by proactively managing life-limited parts and configuration changes.
  • Optimized Maintenance: Enhance maintenance planning and execution by aligning it with actual asset configurations.
  • Enhanced Decision-Making: Leverage accurate configuration data to support strategic asset management decisions.

Use Cases​

Maximo Aviation is ideal for industries where asset configuration is critical to safety, performance, and compliance, including:

  • Aerospace and Defense
  • Rail and Transit
  • Marine
  • Space and Satellite Technologies
  • Oil and Gas
