Complex Assets Accelerator

Overview​

IBM Maximo Manage Complex Assets is an accelerator that provides organizations with comprehensive capabilities to manage complex, high-value assets where safety and regulatory compliance are critical success factors.

It delivers an integrated solution for the entire complex asset maintenance lifecycle from entry into service until retirement.

The Complex Assets accelerator leverages the proven capabilities of the Maximo Manage Aviation solution but employs industry-agnostic terminology, making it suitable for organizations in industries such as:

  • Rail - Rolling stock, locomotives, and rail infrastructure
  • Marine - Vessels, ships, and maritime equipment
  • Space - Spacecraft, satellites, and space technology components
  • Defense - Military vehicles and equipment

The Complex Assets accelerator was officially released on November 1, 2024, and is available through the Maximo Application Suite Accelerator Catalog.

Key Features and Capabilities​

Asset Configuration Management​

The accelerator provides comprehensive configuration management functionality:

  • Component Hierarchies: Manage complete asset build structures with full traceability of serial numbers and parts
  • Real-Time Validation: The Build Data Interpreter (BDI) continuously validates that the asset's current configuration conforms to design and regulatory rules
  • Lifecycle History: Track every install/remove action with historical views for audits and investigations
  • Dynamic Maintenance Plans: Maintenance schedules automatically adapt based on current configurations

Technical Records Management​

  • Technical Publications: Record and manage technical publications received from various parties
  • Engineering Assessment Orders: Formally assess technical publications and plan required engineering orders
  • Configuration Rules: Define and enforce configuration rules across asset models
  • Compliance Tracking: Ensure all assets meet regulatory and design requirements

Maintenance Planning and Execution​

  • Work Packages: Bundle work orders of different types for efficient maintenance execution
  • Operator Maintenance Programs (OMP): Define and manage agreed maintenance programs for asset types
  • Master Task Cards: Create templates for preventive maintenance tasks
  • Maintenance Task Library: Import and manage maintenance tasks from external sources

Fleet and Asset Management​

  • Fleet Management: Organize configuration-managed assets into logical groups
  • New Asset Assemblies: Use templates for creating complex asset assemblies
  • MRO Location Management: Manage maintenance, repair, and overhaul facilities
  • Asset Roles and Capabilities: Define roles and capabilities associated with assets

Service Provider Capabilities​

  • Customer Management: Manage both external and internal customers
  • Customer Agreements: Define pricing and billing schedules for services
  • Bill Review and Customer Billing: Comprehensive billing workflow for service providers
  • Response Plans: Automate responses to tickets and work orders at different process stages

Quality and Compliance​

  • Audit and Survey Management: Conduct and track audits and surveys
  • Defect Tracking: Record and manage quality defects
  • Regulatory Compliance: Maintain electronic references of regulations and standards
  • Operating Procedures: Document key points of standards and procedures

Solution Benefits​

Operational Excellence​

  • Improved Asset Uptime: Proactive maintenance planning reduces unplanned downtime
  • Configuration Control: Ensure what's installed is exactly what should be installed
  • Optimized Maintenance: Bundle work efficiently when assets enter repair facilities
  • Resource Optimization: Better scheduling and resource allocation

Compliance and Safety​

  • Regulatory Compliance: Track and maintain compliance with industry regulations
  • Safety Management: Integrated safety and quality management capabilities
  • Audit Trail: Complete history of all configuration changes and maintenance activities
  • Certification Tracking: Manage certifications for locations, companies, and personnel

Financial Performance​

  • Cost Visibility: Track performance and costs across multiple assets using component codes
  • Billing Automation: Streamline billing processes for service providers
  • Warranty Management: Track and manage warranties for assets and items
  • Inventory Optimization: Better management of rotables, consumables, and shelf-life items

Decision Support​

  • Performance Tracking: Monitor asset and component performance over time
  • Lifecycle Visibility: Complete view of asset lifecycle from entry to retirement
  • Data-Driven Insights: Make informed decisions based on comprehensive asset data

Terminology Differences​

One of the key features of the Complex Assets accelerator is the use of industry-agnostic terminology instead of aviation-specific terms. This makes the solution accessible to a broader range of industries while maintaining the same powerful functionality.

The Complex Assets Accelerator employs different terminology than that of Maximo Aviation. However the applications and capabilities are identical to Maximo Aviation.

For a comprehensive list of terminology differences, refer to the IBM Support documentation linked in the References section below:

https://www.ibm.com/support/pages/maximo-manage-complex-assets-and-aviation-terminology-differences

Prerequisites and Requirements​

System Requirements​

  • Maximo Manage: Version 9.0.x or higher
  • Aviation Component: Must be enabled as a prerequisite
  • Maximo Application Suite: Compatible with MAS 9.0+

Compatibility Note: The Complex Assets accelerator has compatibility restrictions with other Industry Solutions and Add-ons. Consult the Maximo Manage Compatibility Matrix before installation.

Installation​

The accelerator is provided as a free downloadable customization archive that can be deployed using the standard Maximo Manage customization process.

Installation Steps:

  1. Ensure Maximo Manage 9.0.x is installed
  2. Enable the Aviation component
  3. Download the Complex Assets customization archive
  4. Deploy using the standard Manage customization process

For detailed installation instructions, refer to the Maximo Manage documentation.

Use Cases​

Rail Industry​

  • Rolling Stock Management: Track configurations of locomotives and rail cars
  • Component Tracking: Manage bogies, engines, and other critical components
  • Maintenance Scheduling: Plan maintenance based on usage and regulatory requirements
  • Fleet Optimization: Optimize fleet utilization and maintenance windows

Marine Industry​

  • Vessel Management: Manage ship configurations and equipment
  • Dry Dock Planning: Plan and execute major maintenance during dry dock periods
  • Certification Tracking: Track vessel certifications and compliance
  • Component Lifecycle: Manage engines, navigation systems, and other critical equipment

Space Technology​

  • Spacecraft Configuration: Maintain detailed configuration records for spacecraft
  • Component Traceability: Track every component with complete serial number history
  • Mission Planning: Coordinate maintenance with mission schedules
  • Regulatory Compliance: Ensure compliance with space industry regulations
